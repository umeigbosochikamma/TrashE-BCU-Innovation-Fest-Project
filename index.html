About Us

Built at BCU, for BCU

TrashE was born in the halls of Birmingham City University's Steamhouse — a place built for bold ideas and innovative thinkers. We're a group of students who looked at overflowing bins on campus and thought: there has to be a smarter way.

So we built one. TrashE combines IoT sensor technology, QR-powered user identification, and a gamified rewards system to transform recycling into something people genuinely want to do.

Our mission is simple: make sustainability accessible, rewarding, and measurable. When people can see the real impact of their choices in real time, they make better ones.

TrashE is our contribution to a greener campus — and we don't plan to stop here.

Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a TrashE account?+
Click "Sign Up" in the top navigation, enter your name, BCU email, and a password. Your account is created instantly and your points are stored securely — accessible from any device, anywhere.
My scan didn't register — what do I do?+
Ensure you're within 10cm of the QR code and your camera is focused. If it persists, email us at hello@trashe.co.uk and we'll credit your account within 24 hours.
How many points do I earn per item?+
You earn 10 points per correctly sorted item, 15 points for harder-to-recycle materials like glass, and bonus points on double-point Fridays. Incorrect sorting still earns 2 points — we reward the effort, but correct sorting is key.
How do I redeem my rewards?+
Log into your TrashE dashboard, go to the Rewards section, and tap 'Redeem' on any unlocked voucher. You'll receive a unique code to your BCU email, valid for 30 days at participating campus locations.
Do points expire?+
Points are valid for 12 months from the date they're earned. You'll receive an email reminder 30 days before any points expire, giving you plenty of time to use them.
Which items can I recycle in TrashE bins?+
TrashE bins accept paper and cardboard, plastic bottles and containers, glass bottles and jars, aluminium cans and tins, and general waste. Flatten cardboard and rinse containers for maximum sensor accuracy.
